# Overview

## The question

How do you create **transparency** and **credibility** when you cannot share the data you use?

- What kind of data *can* you share?
- Strategies for data **collection**, data **use**, and data **sharing**
- Relevant if you **collect your own data**, or if you use **confidential data**

## Roadmap

1. When can you share data -- and how do you know you cannot?
2. How do you know you *have* data you cannot share?
3. Methods to make non-shareable data shareable
4. Methods to publish data securely, with controls
