## How do you know you cannot share data?

- **Licenses and data use agreements**
  - Example 1: [PSID](https://simba.isr.umich.edu/U/CondUse.aspx)
  - Example 2: Demographic and Health Surveys ([DHS](https://dhsprogram.com/data/dataset_admin/login_main.cfm))
- **Institutional review board** (IRB) restrictions
- **Confidentiality agreements**
- **Legal restrictions** (🇪🇺 GDPR, [🇧🇷 LGPD](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=BR))
